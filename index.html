Our Story

Where Flavour Meets Heritage

Savanna Grill was born from a simple belief: that East Africa's ingredients, produce, and culinary traditions deserve a world-class stage. We source directly from Kenyan farms, work with local spice merchants, and build every dish around what the season gives us. No imported substitutes. No shortcuts. Just honest, generous cooking in a space that makes you want to stay a little longer.

Chef's Signature

Nyama Choma Platter

Slow-roasted over open coals for six hours, our signature Nyama Choma is seasoned with a dry rub of Kenyan herbs, Rift Valley rock salt, and roasted garlic. Served with kachumbari, ugali, and a smoky peanut sauce. Kenya's greatest culinary tradition — elevated.
